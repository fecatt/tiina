function canNormalize(parts:Point[][],doc:Doc,self:number):boolean{
  // Only two collinear parts joined at a shared endpoint, no component pin at that shared point.
  if(parts.length!==2)return false;
  const [a,b]=parts, shared=[a.at(-1)!,b[0],a[0],b.at(-1)!].find(p=>parts.some(x=>same(x[0],p))&&parts.some(x=>same(x.at(-1)!,p)));

## Behavior notes

**Original behavior (bug):** `translateSelection` used `onWire` over every vertex, so a wire passing through an interior bend of a selected wire was treated as a cut point, emitting a one-bridge-per-vertex phantom branch when only the two true endpoints carry electrical contact. `addConnectedWire` unconditionally re-joined touching endpoints, silently merging into multiway junctions and component pins.

**Patched behavior:** groups connect only at endpoint-to-endpoint contact (matches native electrical semantics); the phantom-branch class of bug is eliminated. Browser-authored collinear wire pairs (fresh `w…` uid, single interior split, no third record or pin at the shared point) normalize into one selectable wire; imported/opaque wires and any contact at a real multiway junction or component pin are left verbatim, so no opaque data or connectivity is altered.
